After you uninstall

Clear leftover files

Deleted apps often leave folders behind. Here's how to find and safely remove them — plus an honest note on what's genuinely safe to touch.

Golden rule: only delete a folder if its name clearly matches an app you already uninstalled. When unsure, leave it — a stray 20 MB folder is cheaper than a broken app.

Find orphaned folders (Android)

  • 1
    Open your Files app (or Files by Google) and go to Internal storage.
  • 2
    Open Android/data and Android/obb. Folders here are named by app, like com.zynga.words. If the app is gone, that folder is safe to delete.
  • 3
    Back in Internal storage, look for app-named folders (a game or camera app often makes one). Match the name to something you removed before deleting.
  • 4
    Empty the .thumbnails folder inside DCIM — it rebuilds itself and can hold hundreds of MB.

The safe one-tap cleanups

  • A
    App cache: Settings → Apps → pick an app → Storage → Clear cache. Frees space, keeps your logins & data.
  • B
    Downloads folder: old PDFs, installers and images pile up here. Sort by size and clear what you don't need.
  • C
    Messaging media: WhatsApp / Messages save every received photo & video. Use their in-app Storage tool to bulk-delete.
iPhone? There's no open file system to browse. Use Settings → General → iPhone Storage — it lists every app by size and offers "Offload" (frees space, keeps data) per app.
